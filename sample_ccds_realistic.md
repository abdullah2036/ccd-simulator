## Sample 1: Crude Distillation Unit (CDU) - Overhead System

```
CORROSION CONTROL DOCUMENT
API RP 970 Compliant

UNIT: Crude Distillation Unit - Overhead System
DOCUMENT ID: CCD-CDU-OH-001
REVISION: 2.1
DATE: October 2024

PROCESS DESCRIPTION:
The crude distillation overhead system handles light hydrocarbon vapors, water, and corrosive species (HCl, H2S, NH3) from the crude tower top. Operating temperature ranges from 100°C to 140°C with pressure at 1.2-1.5 bar.

MATERIALS OF CONSTRUCTION:
- Overhead Line: Carbon Steel API 5L Grade B
- Condenser Tubes: Admiralty Brass
- Receiver Drum: Carbon Steel SA-516 Grade 70
- Piping: Carbon Steel ASTM A106 Grade B

WALL THICKNESS SPECIFICATIONS:
Initial Thickness: 12.7 mm
Minimum Acceptable Thickness: 6.4 mm
Corrosion Allowance: 3.0 mm

OPERATING CONDITIONS:
Temperature: 120°C (Design: 150°C)
Pressure: 1.4 bar (Design: 5 bar)
Design Life: 20 years
pH: 5.5-7.0 (controlled)
H2S Concentration: 50-150 ppm
HCl Concentration: 10-30 ppm

COATING/PROTECTION:
Coating: None (bare carbon steel)
Corrosion Mitigation: Filming amine injection at 25 ppm
Neutralizing Agent: Ammonia injection to maintain pH > 5.5

DAMAGE MECHANISMS (API 571):
Primary: Ammonium Chloride Corrosion
Secondary: Wet H2S Corrosion
Tertiary: HCl Acid Corrosion

CRITICAL RELIABILITY VARIABLES:
- Overhead temperature (below water dew point)
- Chloride content in crude
- H2S content
- Wash water pH and rate
- Amine injection rate

INTEGRITY OPERATING WINDOWS:
- Overhead temperature: 110-130°C (Alert: <115°C)
- pH: 5.5-7.0 (Alert: <6.0, >6.8)
- Chlorides: <50 ppm (Alert: >40 ppm)
- Amine residual: 15-35 ppm (Alert: <20 ppm)

INSPECTION STRATEGY:
Frequency: Annual ultrasonic thickness monitoring
Critical Monitoring Locations: 15 CMLs established
Last Inspection: March 2024
Maximum Corrosion Rate Observed: 0.8 mm/year

FAILURE HISTORY:
2019: Pinhole leak in overhead line due to under-deposit corrosion
2021: Salt deposition in condenser tubes, cleaned

RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Maintain strict pH control above 6.0
- Monitor chloride content in desalted crude
- Consider upgrade to 316 SS for high-risk sections
- Increase wash water rate during high-chloride crude processing
```

## Sample 2: Sour Gas Pipeline - Offshore Platform

```
CORROSION CONTROL DOCUMENT
NACE/API Standard Compliant

ASSET: Offshore Gas Export Pipeline Section B-204
DOCUMENT ID: CCD-PIPE-B204-003
REVISION: 3.0
DATE: September 2024

PIPELINE DESCRIPTION:
12-inch diameter offshore gas export pipeline transporting sour gas from wellhead platform to onshore processing facility. Total length: 45 km, water depth: 35-120 meters.

MATERIALS SPECIFICATION:
Material: Carbon Steel API 5L X65 PSL2
Manufacturing: Seamless pipe, double-jointed
Wall Thickness: 15.9 mm
Minimum Wall Thickness: 8.0 mm

EXTERNAL COATING:
Type: 3-layer Polyethylene (3LPE)
Thickness: 3.5 mm
Application Standard: ISO 21809-1
Coating Condition: Good (last survey 2023)

CATHODIC PROTECTION:
Type: Sacrificial Aluminum Anodes
Design Life: 25 years
Anode Spacing: 500 meters
Last Survey: August 2024 (all readings within specification)

INTERNAL CONDITIONS:
Service: Sour gas with condensate
H2S Partial Pressure: 0.8 bar
CO2 Partial Pressure: 0.3 bar
Operating Temperature: 65°C
Operating Pressure: 85 bar
Design Life: 30 years

CORROSION MITIGATION:
Primary: Continuous corrosion inhibitor injection
Inhibitor Type: Film-forming quaternary ammonium compound
Injection Rate: 150 ppm
Backup: Batch treatment capability

DAMAGE MECHANISMS:
Primary: Wet H2S Corrosion (Sulfide Stress Cracking)
Secondary: CO2 Corrosion (Sweet Corrosion)
Tertiary: Erosion-Corrosion at elbows

CRITICAL PROCESS VARIABLES:
- H2S partial pressure
- Water production rate
- Operating temperature
- Flow velocity
- Inhibitor residual concentration

INTEGRITY OPERATING WINDOWS:
- H2S: <1.0 bar (Alert: >0.9 bar)
- Temperature: 50-75°C (Alert: >70°C)
- Inhibitor residual: 80-200 ppm (Alert: <100 ppm)
- Water cut: <5% (Alert: >4%)

INSPECTION/MONITORING:
Method: Intelligent pigging (MFL + UT)
Frequency: Every 5 years
Last ILI: April 2023
Corrosion Rate: 0.05-0.15 mm/year (within acceptable range)
Coupon Monitoring: Quarterly at injection point

RISK ASSESSMENT:
Consequence: High (offshore location, environmental sensitivity)
Probability: Low (effective inhibition program)
Overall Risk: Medium

RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Continue current inhibition program
- Monitor H2S levels closely during well interventions
- Plan replacement by 2048 (end of design life)
- Consider upgrade to CRA clad pipe for future extensions
```

## Sample 3: Refinery Amine System - Moderate Risk

```
CORROSION CONTROL DOCUMENT

UNIT: Amine Treating Unit - Rich/Lean Amine System
FACILITY: Refinery Gas Processing Unit 3
DOCUMENT ID: CCD-AMINE-GPU3-012
DATE: November 2024

PROCESS OVERVIEW:
MDEA (Methyldiethanolamine) system for H2S and CO2 removal from refinery fuel gas. System includes absorber, regenerator, heat exchangers, and circulation pumps.

EQUIPMENT SPECIFICATIONS:

Rich Amine Piping:
- Material: 316 Stainless Steel
- Size: 8-inch Schedule 40
- Wall Thickness: 8.2 mm
- Minimum Thickness: 5.0 mm
- Design Life: 25 years

Lean Amine Piping:
- Material: Carbon Steel ASTM A106
- Size: 8-inch Schedule 80
- Wall Thickness: 12.7 mm
- Minimum Thickness: 7.0 mm
- Coating: Phenolic epoxy internal lining

OPERATING CONDITIONS:
Rich Amine Temperature: 45°C
Lean Amine Temperature: 85°C
System Pressure: 35 bar
Amine Concentration: 50 wt% MDEA
Acid Gas Loading: 0.35 mol/mol
Design Life: 25 years

CORROSION PROTECTION:
- Amine solution quality control (low HSS)
- Heat stable salt removal unit
- Oxygen scavenger injection
- Corrosion inhibitor: Phosphate-based, 200 ppm

DAMAGE MECHANISMS:
Primary: Amine Stress Corrosion Cracking (in rich amine)
Secondary: Erosion-Corrosion (high velocity areas)
Tertiary: Carbonate Stress Corrosion Cracking
Quaternary: Hydrogen Induced Cracking

CRITICAL VARIABLES:
- Heat stable salt concentration
- Amine degradation products
- Operating temperature
- Flow velocity
- Oxygen content

INTEGRITY OPERATING WINDOWS:
- HSS: <1.0 wt% (Alert: >0.8 wt%)
- Lean amine temperature: 80-90°C (Alert: >88°C)
- Oxygen: <10 ppb (Alert: >8 ppb)
- Iron content: <50 ppm (Alert: >40 ppm)
- Flow velocity: <3 m/s (Alert: >2.8 m/s)

MONITORING PROGRAM:
- Monthly amine solution analysis
- Quarterly corrosion coupon retrieval
- Annual ultrasonic thickness survey (50 CMLs)
- Bi-annual hardness testing for HIC susceptibility

INSPECTION RESULTS:
Last Survey: June 2024
Maximum Corrosion Rate: 0.25 mm/year (rich amine line)
Average Rate: 0.12 mm/year
Status: Within acceptable limits

HISTORICAL ISSUES:
2020: Cracking found in rich amine line elbow - replaced with 316L SS
2022: HSS excursion due to amine reclaimer malfunction - corrected

CURRENT RECOMMENDATIONS:
- Replace remaining carbon steel rich amine piping with 316 SS
- Install online iron monitoring system
- Increase amine reclaimer capacity to maintain HSS <0.5%
- Consider upgrade to corrosion-resistant alloy for regenerator overhead
```

## Sample 4: Subsea Pipeline - Excellent Design (Low Risk)

```
CORROSION CONTROL DOCUMENT

ASSET: Subsea Gas Export Pipeline Delta-7
PROJECT: North Sea Development Phase 3
DOCUMENT ID: CCD-SUBSEA-D7-001
ISSUE DATE: August 2024

PIPELINE DATA:
Total Length: 28 kilometers
Water Depth: 85-150 meters
Diameter: 16 inches (406 mm)
Material: Super Duplex Stainless Steel UNS S32750
Wall Thickness: 18.3 mm
Minimum Design Thickness: 9.0 mm
Design Life: 40 years

MATERIAL PROPERTIES:
PREN: >40 (excellent pitting resistance)
Yield Strength: 550 MPa minimum
Corrosion Allowance: 1.5 mm
Fatigue Design: DNV-OS-F101 compliant

EXTERNAL PROTECTION:
Primary: 3-layer Polypropylene (3LPP) coating
Thickness: 4.0 mm
Impact Protection: Concrete weight coating at spans
Cathodic Protection: Aluminum anode bracelet arrays
Anode Life: 50 years (overdesign for redundancy)

INTERNAL SERVICE:
Fluid: Natural gas with condensate
H2S: <100 ppm
CO2: 2.5 mol%
Chlorides: 50,000 ppm (seawater intrusion scenario)
Temperature: 40°C
Pressure: 180 bar
Water Cut: <0.5%

CORROSION CONTROL STRATEGY:
Primary: Material selection (Super Duplex eliminates internal corrosion)
Secondary: 3LPP coating (external protection)
Tertiary: CP system (external protection backup)
Quaternary: Biocide treatment (prevent MIC)

DAMAGE MECHANISMS CONSIDERED:
- Chloride Pitting: Not expected (PREN >40)
- Stress Corrosion Cracking: Not expected at operating temperature
- Sulfide Stress Cracking: Not applicable (Super Duplex immune)
- Hydrogen Embrittlement: Not expected
- Microbiologically Influenced Corrosion: Controlled by biocide

INTEGRITY OPERATING WINDOWS:
- Operating Temperature: 30-50°C (Alert: N/A - wide margin)
- Chloride Ion: <100,000 ppm (Alert: N/A - material suitable)
- pH: 4-9 (Alert: N/A - resistant material)
- H2S: <500 ppm (Alert: >400 ppm as precaution)

INSPECTION STRATEGY:
- Baseline ILI: Completed 2025 (post-commissioning)
- Routine ILI: Every 10 years
- External CP survey: Every 3 years
- ROV coating survey: Every 5 years
- Estimated Service Life: 60+ years (conservative)

RISK ASSESSMENT:
Material Selection: Excellent
Coating System: Excellent
CP Design: Excellent
Overall Risk: Very Low

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY:
Conservative material selection eliminates internal corrosion risk. Multiple barriers approach ensures 40+ year service life with minimal intervention.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
- No immediate actions required
- Maintain biocide treatment program
- Continue periodic CP surveys as planned
- System suitable for life extension beyond design life
```
